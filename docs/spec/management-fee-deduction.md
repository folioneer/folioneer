# Business Rules — Management Fee Deduction (FEE)

## Context

A Management Fee Deduction records an ongoing fund/ETF management fee (TER / ongoing charges) that is paid **not in cash but by periodically reducing the held quantity** of the asset. It is the quantity-_reducing_ sibling of the Free Share Distribution (FSD): where FSD adds shares at no cost, a fee deduction removes shares with no cash movement. The feature covers a **recurring Fee Schedule** — a percentage rate applied per period, configured per (account, asset) — and **one-off** manual deductions for ad-hoc charges. It touches the `account` bounded context (Transaction, Holding, the Account aggregate root) and reuses the chronological-replay machinery (TRX-031/036). Quantities are i64 micro-units ([ADR-001](../adr/001-use-i64-for-monetary-amounts.md)).

The fee's economic cost follows the FSD-mirror model: removing shares while leaving the recorded **cost basis unchanged**, so the average price (VWAP) rises and the fee is fully reflected in the position's value and returns. A derived **Management Fees** figure per holding — shares removed × market price at the deduction date — surfaces the cumulative fee actually paid, so the cost is visible rather than implicit in the VWAP. ("Management Fees" is named to distinguish it from the brokerage-commission `fees` field already on `Transaction`.) Computing it reads recorded prices (asset context) and currency rates (currency context) over the account's holdings, so it is orchestrated by an application use case ([ADR-003](../adr/003-cross-context-use-case-orchestration.md)/[ADR-004](../adr/004-use-cases-inject-services-not-repositories.md)), not the account context alone.

## Entity Definition

### FeeDeduction (new `TransactionType` variant)

A quantity-only event removing shares of a held asset to pay a management fee — no cash, no realized P&L, attributed to the charged asset. Like the Free Share Distribution and Cash Dividend, it is a **new `TransactionType` variant** persisted on the `Transaction` aggregate (not a separate entity); `origin` is a discriminator carried on that transaction.

| Field              | Business meaning                                                                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `transaction_type` | The management-fee variant of `TransactionType`.                                            |
| `account_id`       | The account whose holding the fee is taken from.                                            |
| `asset_id`         | The charged asset — the holding shares are removed from. **Not** the Cash Asset.            |
| `date`             | Business date the fee was applied.                                                          |
| `quantity`         | Number of shares removed (strictly positive, micro-units, fractional allowed as for a Buy). |
| `origin`           | Whether the deduction was recorded manually (one-off) or generated by a Fee Schedule.       |
| `note`             | Optional free-text note.                                                                    |

> A fee deduction carries **no monetary amount, no unit price, no exchange rate, and no cash leg**.

### FeeSchedule

A recurring rule that generates fee deductions for one (account, asset) holding at a fixed cadence.

| Field                 | Business meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `account_id`          | The account the schedule applies to.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `asset_id`            | The charged asset.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `annual_rate`         | The annual fee rate (e.g. TER) as a percentage; the per-period removal is this rate scaled to the frequency.                                                                                                                                 |
| `frequency`           | How often a deduction is generated (see FEE-034).                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `start_date`          | The first business date from which deductions are generated.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `end_date`            | Optional date after which no further deductions are generated.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `active`              | Whether the schedule currently generates deductions.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `last_applied_period` | The period-boundary **date** of the most recent generated deduction — the catch-up cursor (FEE-043). Unset until the first deduction is generated. Travels with the schedule between devices and only ever moves forward (CFR-043, CFR-044). |

## Business Rules

### Eligibility and Initiation (010–019)

**FEE-010 — One-off entry point (frontend)**: The one-off fee flow is initiated from the Account Details header's consolidated "Record" menu (cross-amends DIV-012's menu enumeration), via a "Management fee" item that opens the one-off fee modal (FEE-020). The charged asset is chosen inside the modal.

**FEE-011 — Schedule entry point (frontend)**: A recurring Fee Schedule is created and managed from a per-holding-row "Manage fee" action on the Account Details holdings table, with the charged asset pre-selected from that row.

**FEE-012 — Eligibility (backend)**: A fee deduction — one-off or generated — may apply only to an `(account, asset)` pair with an active holding (`quantity > 0`) that is not a Cash Asset. The action is rejected with a specific error when the account is unknown, the asset is unknown, the asset is not currently held in that account, or the asset is a Cash Asset.

### One-off Fee Deduction (020–029)

**FEE-020 — One-off form fields (frontend)**: The Record-management-fee form accepts an **asset selector** listing the account's active (`quantity > 0`), non-cash holdings, `date` (default: today), the fee itself, and an optional `note`. The fee is entered in one of two modes chosen by a toggle (default: percentage): the **percentage of the holding to remove** (strictly positive, at most `100%`), converted to a share quantity at record time (FEE-022), or the **resulting quantity** — the quantity the holding should hold after the fee, as of that date (FEE-028). There is no amount, unit-price, exchange-rate, or fees input — no money moves.

**FEE-021 — Input validation (frontend + backend)**: Exactly one of the percentage and the resulting quantity is provided. The percentage must parse as a strictly positive value of at most `100%`; the resulting quantity must be zero or more and strictly below the quantity held as of the `date` (FEE-028); the `date` must be a well-formed ISO 8601 calendar date that is not in the future and not older than the TRX lower bound (TRX-020). The frontend validates inline what it can check alone — exactly one mode, a parsable value, the percentage bounds, a resulting quantity of zero or more, the date bounds — and disables submit until valid; the upper bound of the resulting quantity needs the quantity held as of the `date`, so it comes from the preview (FEE-029). The backend re-validates, converts the percentage to a removed quantity (FEE-022), and rejects with explicit error variants.

**FEE-022 — Recording effect (backend)**: Recording a one-off fee deduction, within a single Unit of Work ([ADR-006](../adr/006-unit-of-work.md)): (a) in percentage mode, converts the entered percentage to a removed quantity = `floor(holding_quantity_as_of(date) × percentage)` — a computed removal of zero is rejected — and decreases the holding's `quantity` by it (in resulting-quantity mode the removed quantity comes from FEE-028); (b) leaves the holding's total cost basis **unchanged**; (c) persists the FeeDeduction (`origin = manual`) attributed to the charged asset; (d) leaves the account's Cash Holding **untouched**. All steps commit together or all roll back.

**FEE-023 — VWAP concentration and no realized P&L (backend)**: Removing shares with the cost basis unchanged raises the average price to `floor(cost_basis / new_quantity)` (concentrated, following the TRX-026 floor convention). A fee deduction never contributes to realized P&L — the cost surfaces as the position's reduced value, not as a realized loss.

**FEE-024 — No effect on recorded market price (backend)**: Recording a deduction **does not create or modify any `AssetPrice` record** — mirroring FSD-024.

**FEE-025 — In-flight, success, and error feedback (frontend)**: While the request is in progress the submit button is disabled and shows a spinner. On success the form closes and a snackbar confirms "Management fee recorded". On validation failure or backend rejection the form stays open with an inline error adjacent to the offending field.

**FEE-026 — Reactivity (frontend + backend)**: Recording a deduction publishes the existing `TransactionUpdated` event; the Account Details view re-fetches (ACD-039), so the holding row's reduced quantity, raised average price, the Management Fees figure, and recomputed performance appear without a manual refresh.

**FEE-027 — Replay integrity on recording (backend)**: Recording (or editing) a one-off fee deduction is rejected with a specific error if, in chronological replay for the `(account, asset)` pair, the removed quantity would leave a later Sell (or any quantity-consuming event) unable to source its quantity — a downstream oversell. The frontend surfaces the rejection inline (FEE-025). The quantity-reducing direction places the oversell risk at **recording** time, not deletion (FEE-063): removing shares can starve a later sell, whereas restoring them never can.

**FEE-028 — Resulting-quantity mode (backend)**: When the fee is entered as a resulting quantity, the removed quantity = `holding_quantity_as_of(date) − resulting quantity`, recorded exactly — no percentage conversion and no rounding — and the holding's `quantity` decreases by it. A resulting quantity below zero is rejected; one that is not strictly below the quantity held as of the `date` is rejected with a message stating that quantity. A resulting quantity of zero removes the whole holding, as a 100% fee does. Apart from how the removed quantity is obtained, the recording is FEE-022 (b)–(d), FEE-023 and FEE-027: the deduction is an ordinary one-off management fee (`origin = manual`) and counts in the Management Fees figures (FEE-052/053). This mode differs from the interest form's quantity mode (INT-021), which takes the credited quantity itself: here the user types what the holding should hold afterwards.

**FEE-029 — Removal preview (frontend + backend)**: In resulting-quantity mode the form shows, under the field, what the entry means before submit: the quantity held as of the `date`, the quantity that will be removed, and the share of the holding it represents = `removed ÷ held as of the date`, in micro-percent, floored. The frontend asks for the preview each time the asset, the `date` or the resulting quantity changes, once all three pass their local checks (FEE-021); a preview for an earlier entry is discarded. The backend computes the three figures and reproduces the rejections of FEE-012 (eligibility), FEE-077 (account parameter), FEE-021 (date bounds) and FEE-028 (resulting-quantity bounds); it writes nothing, and the replay-integrity guard (FEE-027) stays with the recording. The frontend only formats the figures, shows a rejection inline under the field, and keeps submit disabled until a preview exists.

### Fee Schedule Configuration (030–039)

**FEE-030 — Create schedule (frontend)**: From the holding-row "Manage fee" action (FEE-011), the user sets the `annual_rate` (percentage `> 0`), the `frequency`, a `start_date` (default: today), and an optional `end_date`.

**FEE-031 — One schedule per holding (backend)**: At most one Fee Schedule may exist per `(account, asset)` pair. Creating a second is rejected with a specific error; the existing schedule is edited instead (FEE-060). <!-- AI-Decision: single schedule per holding — duplicate rejected -->

**FEE-032 — Schedule validation (frontend + backend)**: `annual_rate` must be strictly positive and below a sane cap (`< 100%` per year); `start_date` must be a well-formed ISO date; `end_date`, if present, must be on or after `start_date`; the asset must be held and non-cash (FEE-012).

**FEE-033 — Schedule persistence (backend)**: Creating a schedule persists it but does **not** itself remove shares — deductions are produced only by generation (FEE-04x).

**FEE-034 — Frequency options (frontend + backend)**: A schedule's `frequency` is one of **monthly**, **quarterly**, or **annually**; the per-period removal scales the annual rate to the frequency — `periods_per_year` is 12, 4, or 1 respectively (FEE-041).

### Recurring Generation (040–049)

**FEE-040 — Lazy catch-up on app open (backend)**: On application startup, for every `active` schedule, the system generates a FeeDeduction for each **completed** due period whose boundary date is on or after `start_date` and strictly after `last_applied_period`, up to the most recently completed period as of today, in chronological order. The in-progress current period is **not** charged until its boundary has passed, so no deduction is ever future-dated (FEE-021, TRX-020). A schedule whose `start_date` is in the past therefore **backfills** every elapsed completed period on first open. No always-on scheduler is involved. When the installation shares its portfolio with other devices, the catch-up runs after the launch sync (SYN-060), so it sees the merged ledger. <!-- AI-Decision: lazy materialization on app open — ADR-018 -->

**FEE-041 — Per-period removal amount (backend)**: Each generated deduction removes `floor(holding_quantity × annual_rate ÷ periods_per_year)` shares, computed against the holding quantity **as of that period** — deductions are applied sequentially, so each one reduces the base for the next.

**FEE-042 — Period date convention (backend)**: Each generated deduction is dated at its period boundary (e.g. the last calendar day of the month for a monthly cadence).

**FEE-043 — Idempotency via cursor (backend)**: Generation advances `last_applied_period` to the boundary date of the period just generated (or skipped, FEE-047). A deduction is never generated twice for the same period, and **deleting a generated deduction does not cause it to be regenerated** — the cursor is the source of truth, not the presence of the transaction. When the schedule is shared between devices, the cursor merges to the most advanced value and never moves backwards (CFR-043, CFR-044).

**FEE-044 — Catch-up across long absences (backend)**: If the application was not opened for several periods, all missed periods are generated at once on the next open, each dated at its own period boundary and applied sequentially (FEE-041).

**FEE-045 — End and inactivity (backend)**: No deductions are generated for periods whose boundary date is after `end_date`, nor while the schedule is inactive (FEE-061).

**FEE-046 — Generated deductions enter replay (backend)**: Generated FeeDeductions participate in the chronological replay (TRX-031/036) at their `date` exactly like one-off deductions; downstream sells and valuation reflect them.

**FEE-047 — Generation replay-safety (backend)**: If a generated deduction would cause a downstream oversell (FEE-027) — e.g. a backfilled period dated before an already-recorded Sell — that period's deduction is **skipped**: no FeeDeduction is created, the cursor still advances past it (FEE-043), and the skip is logged. Automatic generation never blocks app startup.

**FEE-048 — Deterministic deduction identity (backend)**: A generated deduction's identity is derived from the holding it charges — the **account and asset** — and its period boundary (never from the schedule's local key), so generating the same period for the same holding — on the same device, or on two devices that later merge (CFR-034, CFR-043) — always yields the same transaction, and two generations of one period converge to a single deduction rather than charging the holding twice. One-off deductions (FEE-02x) keep a freshly generated identity.

### Cost Basis, Valuation & Management Fees Display (050–059)

**FEE-050 — Cost-basis model (backend)**: A fee deduction reduces `quantity` and leaves the recorded cost basis **unchanged**. The position's value and unrealized P&L drop by the market value of the removed shares; later sells compute realized P&L against the raised average price. The fee is thus fully reflected in returns without a separate realized-loss line.

**FEE-051 — Fee value derivation (backend)**: The fee value of a deduction = `quantity_removed × carry-forward market price as of the deduction date` (PRF-022 carry-forward lookup), converted to the account currency (FXR as-of the deduction date). It is **derived on read**, never stored — consistent with [ADR-013](../adr/013-recompute-account-performance-on-read.md). It is a **historical cost metric** (each deduction valued at its own date's price); it intentionally does not reconcile to the live drop in position value (FEE-050), which uses the current price.

**FEE-052 — Per-holding Management Fees figure (frontend + backend)**: The application computes, per `(account, asset)`, a cumulative **Management Fees** value = the sum of every recorded deduction's fee value (FEE-051), in account currency; the Account Details holdings table displays it as a **Management Fees** column. The backend produces the aggregate and the frontend displays it, mirroring DIV-070.

**FEE-053 — Account-level total Management Fees (frontend + backend)**: The application computes an account-level **total Management Fees** = the sum of the per-holding Management Fees across the account, in account currency, surfaced in the Account Details view header. Backend aggregates, frontend displays, mirroring DIV-073.

**FEE-054 — No price available (frontend + backend)**: If no market price is recorded on or before a deduction's date, that deduction contributes `0` to the Management Fees figure until a qualifying price exists (consistent with valuation degrading to 0, FXR-034). The quantity removal itself is unaffected.

**FEE-055 — Transaction list inclusion (frontend)**: Fee deductions appear in the transaction list (TXL) when filtering by the **charged asset** for that account (cross-amends TXL-023 for the Type label "Management fee", TXL-022 for the `—` placeholder columns). The Quantity column shows the removed quantity as a reduction; the unit-price, total-amount, and Realized P&L columns render `—`.

**FEE-056 — Holding row reflection (frontend)**: After a deduction the holding row shows the reduced quantity and the raised average price; the cost basis figure is unchanged. Unrealized P&L and performance recompute through the existing MKT math with the new quantity.

### Schedule Lifecycle (060–069)

**FEE-060 — Edit schedule (frontend + backend)**: `annual_rate` and `end_date` are editable; changes apply to **future generation only** — already-generated deductions are unchanged. The structural fields `frequency` and `start_date` are **immutable after creation** — the edit surface never accepts them. To change the cadence, the user deletes the schedule (its past deductions remain, FEE-062) and creates a new one — this avoids the partial-period proration ambiguity a mid-stream cadence switch would otherwise introduce. <!-- AI-Decision: rate/end editable; frequency + start_date frozen after first generation; cadence change = delete + recreate -->

**FEE-061 — Pause and reactivate (frontend + backend)**: A schedule can be deactivated; no deductions are generated while inactive. Reactivation resumes generation from the current period — the periods skipped while paused are **not** back-generated. <!-- AI-Decision: pause means no charge for the paused span -->

**FEE-062 — Delete schedule (frontend + backend)**: Deleting a schedule stops all future generation; already-generated deductions **remain** (they are fees actually paid).

**FEE-063 — Edit and delete a recorded deduction (frontend + backend)**: Any recorded fee deduction — one-off or generated — can be edited or deleted through the existing TXL correction/cancel flow. Editable fields: `date`, the removed `quantity` (the neither the entry mode nor what was typed is retained — edits adjust the stored removed quantity), and `note`; the charged `asset_id` is **immutable** on edit (mirroring FSD-040/DIV-040). An edit re-validates the recording replay-integrity guard (FEE-027). Deleting a deduction **restores** the removed shares via replay and is therefore always replay-safe — it can never cause an oversell. Editing or deleting a **generated** deduction does **not** alter the schedule cursor (FEE-043), so it is not regenerated.

**FEE-064 — Schedule reactivity (frontend + backend)**: Creating, editing, pausing, reactivating, or deleting a Fee Schedule publishes a `FeeScheduleUpdated` event; the Account Details view re-fetches on it (per the ACD-039 convention), so the holding-row "Manage fee" affordance and the visible figures refresh without a manual reload.

### Edge Cases & Integration (070–079)

**FEE-070 — Closed or insufficient position (backend)**: When a due deduction's holding quantity is `0`, no deduction is generated for that period and the schedule **stays active** — it charges nothing while nothing is held and resumes automatically if the asset is re-acquired. Because `annual_rate < 100%` per year keeps every per-period removal strictly below the current quantity, a positive holding can always absorb the removal and the result never goes below `0`.

**FEE-071 — Performance treatment (backend)**: A fee deduction is **not an external cash flow** (account performance applies no flow adjustment for it) and **not dividend income** (excluded from `dividends_received`); its impact surfaces through the position's reduced value, the inverse of FSD-070. Its unit reduction **does** enter PRF's as-of-date holding reconstruction through the standard transaction replay (PRF-021).

**FEE-072 — As-of view reflection (frontend + backend)**: In the read-only as-of Account Details view, fee deductions dated on or before the as-of date are reflected in the reconstructed holding. The backend computes the as-of Management Fees figure (summing the fee values of deductions dated on or before the as-of date, FEE-051); the frontend displays it.

**FEE-073 — Foreign-currency holdings (backend)**: For a charged asset whose currency differs from the account currency, the fee value uses the account-currency conversion path (FXR) as of the deduction date, consistent with holding valuation (FXR-042). A deduction with no usable rate contributes `0` to the Management Fees figure (FEE-054, FXR-034).

**FEE-074 — Fee-rate indicator on the holding line (frontend + backend)**: Each holding line carries the annual rate of its **active** fee schedule (FEE-032); a holding with no active schedule carries none — inactive schedules and one-off fees do not count. The live view states today's rate; the as-of view never states one, because the schedule is today's configuration, not part of the historical reconstruction. The rate is shown inside the Management Fees cell after the cumulative amount (e.g. "12,34 · 1,50%"); the cell shows the amount alone when no active schedule exists.

**FEE-075 — Account-level management-fees parameter (frontend + backend)**: the `Account` aggregate carries `management_fees_enabled: bool`. New accounts are created with `false`; the schema migration backfills `true` for every account existing before the parameter (behavior-preserving). The parameter is editable both ways at any time from the account forms (creation and edit).

**FEE-076 — Disabled-state UI (frontend)**: when an account's `management_fees_enabled` is `false`, the account-details view hides every % fee surface: the one-off management-fee header button (FEE-010), the per-row Manage-fee button (FEE-011), the Management Fees column (FEE-052) with its fee-rate indicator (FEE-074), and the header total (FEE-053).

**FEE-077 — Backend enforcement (backend)**: Recording a one-off management fee or creating a fee schedule is rejected with a specific error when the target account's management-fees parameter is off; the account itself owns that rule. Existing schedules remain readable, editable, and deletable regardless of the parameter — only the creation of new fee instruments is rejected, so disabling never traps data.

**FEE-078 — Catch-up pause (backend)**: the startup catch-up (FEE-040) skips every schedule whose account has the parameter disabled, without advancing the `last_applied_period` cursor. Re-enabling the parameter resumes generation with a backfill of the paused periods on the next catch-up run (the schedule models fees the fund charges regardless of app configuration), subject to the FEE-044/047 oversell and zero-quantity guards.

## Workflow

```
One-off fee:
Account Details header → "Record" menu → "Management fee" (FEE-010)
  → modal: asset selector (active non-cash holdings), date (today),
           mode toggle: percentage to remove | resulting quantity, note (FEE-020)
      resulting-quantity mode: each valid entry → backend preview
           held as of date, removed, share of the holding; or a rejection (FEE-029)
  → submit → validate exactly one mode, pct in (0, 100%] or 0 ≤ resulting < held,
             date in bounds, asset held, not cash (FEE-012/021/028)
      backend (single Unit of Work, ADR-006):
        ├─ qty = floor(holding_as_of(date) × pct)               (FEE-022a)
        │   or holding_as_of(date) − resulting quantity, exact  (FEE-028)
        ├─ holding.quantity -= qty
        ├─ cost basis unchanged → average price concentrates    (FEE-022b/023)
        ├─ Cash Holding untouched                               (FEE-022d)
        └─ persist FeeDeduction(origin=manual)                  (FEE-022c)
      reject if a later sell would oversell                     (FEE-027)
      publish TransactionUpdated                                (FEE-026)
  → snackbar "Management fee recorded"; row qty ↓, avg price ↑, Mgmt Fees ↑ (FEE-052/056)

Recurring schedule:
Holding row → "Manage fee" → set annual_rate %, frequency, start, end? (FEE-011/030)
  → persist FeeSchedule (no immediate share removal)            (FEE-033)

App open → for each active schedule (FEE-040):
  for each COMPLETED period after last_applied_period (date) up to today (FEE-040/044):
     remove floor(qty_as_of_period × rate ÷ periods_per_year)   (FEE-041)
     date the deduction at the period boundary                  (FEE-042)
     if it would oversell a later sell → skip the period        (FEE-047)
     advance last_applied_period to the period boundary date    (FEE-043)
  → replay + revalue → Management Fees figures reflect deductions (FEE-046/052)
```

## UX Draft

### Entry Point

- **One-off**: a "Management fee" item in the Account Details header "Record" dropdown (FEE-010), alongside New position, Dividend, and Free shares.
- **Schedule**: a "Manage fee" action on each holding row (FEE-011), opening the schedule modal with the asset pre-selected.

### Main Component

Two small `FormModal`s:

- **One-off fee modal** — asset selector (active non-cash holdings), date (`DateField`, default today), a two-way toggle (Percentage, the default / Resulting quantity), then either the percentage to remove (strictly positive, ≤ 100%) or the quantity held after the fee with its preview line under the field (held as of the date → removed quantity and the share it represents), optional note, Submit / Cancel.
- **Fee schedule modal** — annual rate (% input), frequency selector, start date, optional end date, an active/pause toggle, and Save / Delete for an existing schedule.

The Account Details holdings table gains a **Management Fees** column; the view header shows the **total Management Fees** figure.

### States

- **Idle**: forms with today's date, empty quantity/rate, submit disabled until valid.
- **In-flight**: submit disabled + spinner (FEE-025).
- **Validation / backend error**: inline error adjacent to the field; modal stays open. In resulting-quantity mode a rejected preview is the field's error and replaces the preview line (FEE-029).
- **Success**: modal closes; snackbar; Account Details re-fetches — quantity ↓, average price ↑, Management Fees ↑, performance recomputed.
- **Empty Management Fees**: a holding with no deductions shows `—` (or `0`) in the Management Fees column.

### User Flow

1. User opens Account Details for an account holding 1000 shares of an ETF.
2. User opens the holding row's "Manage fee" action and sets an annual rate of 0.20%, monthly cadence, starting today.
3. Each month, on the next app open, a deduction removes ~0.0167% of the current quantity, dated at month end.
4. The holding row shows the gradually shrinking quantity, a slowly rising average price, and a growing **Management Fees** figure; the account header shows the total Management Fees paid.
5. For an ad-hoc charge, the user instead picks "Management fee" from the header Record menu and records a single percentage removal (e.g. 1% of the holding) — or, to align the holding with the real position, switches to Resulting quantity and types what it should hold (25 held, 24.5 typed: the form shows that 0.5 will be removed).

## Open Questions

Interview decisions (all resolved):

- [x] **Q1 — Frequency set**: monthly / quarterly / annually (FEE-034).
- [x] **Q2 — Backfill on creation**: the first catch-up backfills every due period from `start_date`, including past ones (FEE-040).
- [x] **Q3 — One-off removal basis**: two entry modes — a **percentage** of the holding, converted to a quantity at record time (FEE-020/022, the default), or the **resulting quantity** the holding should hold afterwards (FEE-028) — consistent with the recurring rate model.
- [x] **Q4 — Schedule entry point**: a per-holding-row "Manage fee" action (FEE-011).
- [x] **Q5 — Closed-position behaviour**: skip the period; the schedule stays active and auto-resumes when the asset is re-acquired (FEE-070).
- [x] **One-off entries retained** alongside recurring schedules (for irregular, non-recurring charges).

Resolved in the spec-reviewer round:

- [x] **Oversell direction**: the replay-integrity guard sits on **recording/generation**, not deletion — recording a one-off that would oversell a later sell is rejected (FEE-027); a generated period that would conflict is skipped (FEE-047); deleting a deduction restores shares and is always safe (FEE-063).
- [x] **Naming**: the per-holding/account figure is **Management Fees**, distinct from the brokerage `fees` field on `Transaction` (FEE-052/053).
- [x] **Entity shape**: `FeeDeduction` is a new `TransactionType` variant with an `origin` discriminator (not a standalone entity).
- [x] **Period dating**: only **completed** periods are charged; the in-progress period waits for its boundary, so no deduction is future-dated (FEE-040).
- [x] **Cursor**: `last_applied_period` is the boundary **date** of the last generated period, unset initially, making the first run unambiguous (FEE-043).
- [x] **Frequency immutable**: a schedule's cadence and `start_date` are frozen after the first deduction; changing cadence means delete + recreate, sidestepping mid-stream proration (FEE-060).

A few mechanical defaults remain flagged inline with `<!-- AI-Decision -->` for later review: single schedule per holding (FEE-031); only `annual_rate`/`end_date` editable with `frequency` + `start_date` frozen after first generation (FEE-060); a paused span is not back-generated on reactivation (FEE-061).
