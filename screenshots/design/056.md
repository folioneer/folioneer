- **Moves**: "New asset" opens on the kind (Listed preselected) and the lookup; the form of a listed asset comes after the search, with "Back to the search". The ISIN sits beside the currency, the marketplace beside the ticker.
- **Added**: the Kind choice with one line saying what it means; "Symbol" and "Quoted in" for a crypto asset; a reference proposed from the name for a custom one; a refusal beside the actions naming the existing asset; "also held as …" under the name in the assets table — one other listing in full, more than one as the first followed by "…" with the whole list in the hint (owner, 2026-10-09); "Managed by Folioneer" on a cash row, which has no action.
- **Removed**: "ISIN (optional)" — required when listed, absent otherwise; the marketplace and the class of a crypto asset; the marketplace and the lookup of a custom one; the "reference already used" warning, replaced by the refusal.
- **Same**: the name, the category, the risk level, "Interest bearing", the dialog and its Cancel / Add actions, the table's columns.
- **Open point**: none. The mock-ups' "Marketplace" label is "Exchange" ("Place de cotation"), the confirmed term; in French the kinds read "Nature : Coté / Crypto / Non coté" (owner, 2026-10-09).
