# TD-068 — Editing a dividend shows what a dividend has

- **Moves**: a dividend opens its own "Edit dividend" dialog instead of the generic edit dialog.
- **Added**: the paying asset and the account as a read-only line (DIV-040: neither can change); the total credited, in the account's currency, read from the core.
- **Removed**: the unit price, the fees, the entry-mode toggle and the "use this price as the market price" checkbox; the label "Quantity" becomes "Amount" with its currency.
- **Same**: the date, the note, the exchange rate when the asset and the account differ in currency; a problem shows under its field (TRX-067).
- **Open point**: none.
